Business fundamentals first, machine learning second

Most false alerts come from models built without understanding how money actually moves. My BBA in Finance gives me the grounding in banking products, credit risk and regulation, so I start from the typology (structuring, layering, shell-company flows) and only then choose a model.

I pair that domain view with Python, SQL and Power BI to engineer behavioral features, tune alert thresholds, and document every decision the way an examiner expects: clear rationale, reproducible results, and a defensible audit trail.

Currently focused on reducing false positives in transaction monitoring and applying LLMs to first-draft SAR narratives, always with a human analyst making the final call.
